# Item Checklist

**F1** opens a searchable checklist of every discoverable item in the game —
vanilla and mod-added alike — so you can see what is still missing. Tracked per
world and per player.

It answers two different questions. **Discovered** ticks itself when you pick
something up, and anything you have not seen stays hidden as `???`, so the list
is safe to browse without spoiling yourself. **Owned** counts how many you
actually have right now, across your inventory and your base storage, with a
filter for the frustrating category: things you found once and no longer have.

Left-click a row and a HUD arrow points at every container in your base holding
that item — no more opening twelve chests. Pets are tracked per colour variant,
and so are cattle and paintable furniture.

## Requirements

**CoreLib**, **Mod Settings Menu** and **Complete Tiny Font**, all pulled in
when you subscribe. Complete Tiny Font is a hard one: without it the mod
refuses to load at all rather than render broken text.

Client-side only — nothing to install on a server.
